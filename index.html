Getting my flat’s interiors done in forty days, without the hassle

I wanted my flat to look great, and I didn’t want any hassle. Everything I heard said I couldn’t have both. This is what actually happened, what I valued in the person I worked with, what I honestly can’t judge, and what I’d look for if I did it again.

The flat

I bought a 2 BHK in Aparna Zenon, Hyderabad. My first idea was to sell it. Over time I changed my mind and decided to keep it and live in it myself.

It’s above the 20th floor and faces east. The view is excellent, there’s plenty of air and light, and the balcony is lovely.

Once I decided to move in, two things mattered to me. I wanted the flat to look really good. And I didn’t want any hassle at all.

What I heard before starting

Before choosing anyone, I asked around and read about other people’s experiences. The picture wasn’t encouraging.

  • Interior firms are very good at sales. They say a lot and show impressive designs, but often under-deliver.
  • They quote one to two months and take three to four.
  • Getting close to what you imagined is rare. Something is usually off in colour, quality or design.
  • Many collect 50 to 80 percent of the money upfront, and the delays start after that.
  • The owner ends up doing a lot of follow-ups.
  • Problems with execution and skill on site are common.

Hearing all this, I panicked. It looked like “looks great” and “no hassle” were not going to go together, whoever I chose.

What actually happened

I found the firm through a recommendation in a group I’m part of, and gave them the complete interior work. The project was done end to end in 40 days. Execution was smooth, without hiccups or unnecessary delays, and the team was professional and well coordinated.

The 40 days stands out, but there was more to it than speed. These are the things that stood out to me about the person who ran our project.

What I liked

Honest from day one

At the start, I didn’t fully believe what he told me. Looking back, he was honest about all of it.

Thinks like an owner

He doesn’t come across as commercial. He owns several flats himself that he has rented out, so he knows what a home actually needs and what it doesn’t. He won’t do something that profits him if it would hurt us later.

Real taste

He has excellent taste and high standards when visualising a space. I was sceptical from day one, and honestly, I don’t have much confidence in my own taste. But I trusted him on this.

Clear guidance

He was clear in what he said from the first day, and his suggestions were sound.

Fast execution

On speed, it was excellent: 40 days from start to finish.

What I can’t fully judge

I’d rather be upfront about two things.

Cost

I’m not certain here. Compared with the others I looked at, the pricing felt reasonable at the time.

Material quality

Visually, the result is excellent. Whether every material is top quality, I honestly can’t say. That isn’t a doubt about the work. I just don’t have the expertise to judge materials. If it helps, I can share the names of the material brands that were used.

Would I hire him again?

Yes. If I have another flat in the future, I’d go with him for sure.

If you’re about to start

This is what I’d pay attention to, based on my own experience. It isn’t professional advice. It’s what I’d tell a friend.

  • Notice who tells you what you don’t need

    Someone who talks you out of unnecessary work is usually thinking about your home, not their bill.

  • Check early promises with past clients

    Ask people who have already worked with them whether what they were told at the start held up.

  • See finished homes, not just designs

    If you’re unsure of your own taste, like me, a finished home is the best way to decide whose taste to trust.

  • Get the timeline and payment stages in writing

    Link payments to visible progress rather than paying most of it upfront.

  • List materials and brands in the quote

    This matters most if, like me, you can’t judge materials yourself.

  • Keep the final payment for the snag list

    Walk through the finished flat together, and release the last amount once everything on the list is fixed.
